The program

Free CrossFit. Real community. A shot to thrive.

ThrHIVE is its own nonprofit, separate from the gym, built to do one thing: open the doors of the Hive to people working through recovery. No membership. No cost. Just coaching, community, and a crew that shows up.

Thrive

thrive /THrīv/ verb: to grow. To flourish. To get after it.

+ Hive

hive /hīv/ noun: a teeming multitude. A nod to Utah, the Beehive State.

= ThrHIVE

Free training, a real community, and a whole Hive in your corner.

01

Free training

Free CrossFit training for local drug and alcohol recovery programs in Salt Lake. That's the starting point, and we'll see where this thing goes.

02

Part of the Hive

It's not just workouts. It's bringing people in recovery through our doors as a real part of the Salty Hive community.

03

A true nonprofit

ThrHIVE is a registered 501(c)(3), so every donation that fuels it can be a tax write-off for the donor.

Why ThrHIVE exists

From the clinic to the gym floor.

Before he ever owned a gym, Justin Zizumbo was a social worker. His last job: clinical director of a drug and alcohol outpatient program, one that built health, fitness coaching and CrossFit right into recovery.

“During that time I really had the opportunity to realize what my passion was, and that was helping people inside of a gym.”

He's been doing this full time since 2019, and ever since, he's wanted to bridge the gap between what happens at Salty Hive and what he's always wanted to do: help an underserved population. ThrHIVE is that bridge.

The community they're joining

The Hive way

ThrHIVE opens the door to the same Hive our members love: a community for ANYone willing to consistently work hard, respect those around them, and hold themselves accountable.

01

Community

We cheer for, challenge, and support each other. Every member is known, and no one goes through this journey alone.

02

Hard Work

It's about attitude and effort, not results. Every workout gets scaled so everyone, beginner or seasoned athlete, feels challenged and motivated.

03

Accountability

We get to know your motivations and goals, then hold you to consistency, good movement, and a routine that lasts.
