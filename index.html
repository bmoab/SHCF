What we do

Strong gyms. Stronger communities.

ThrHIVE partners local programs with CrossFit gyms, and covers the cost so it's free for the people who need it.

  1. 01

    Partner

    We team up with local programs serving underserved communities.

  2. 02

    Connect

    We match them with CrossFit gyms ready to coach.

  3. 03

    Fund

    We pay for a 12-week program, so it's free to every participant.

Something bigger, for the people we don't yet get to serve.

Justin Zizumbo, founder
